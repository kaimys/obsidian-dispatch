DispatchOCR

Derived from OCR A by Matthew Skala (2011-12, 2021), based on code by
Richard B. Wales (1988-89) and Tor Lillqvist.
Upstream: https://tsukurimashou.org/ocr.php.en

Matthew Skala places his work in the public domain. The Richard B. Wales
notice it derives from asks that the credit stay intact and that the font
not be sold. The attribution also lives in the font's own name table and in
the comment above the @font-face rule in style.css — keep them together.

Changes in the Dispatch build: added U+00E4 U+00F6 U+00FC (derived from the
capitals), U+25B6 and U+25B7 (drawn); family renamed to DispatchOCR.
Build script: build-font.py (see 06_UX/Dispatch-UX.md).
